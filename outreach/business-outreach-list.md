# Crave Meal Prep Co. — Corporate B2B Outreach List
**Warehouse Base:** Asbury Park, NJ  
**Target Radius:** 15 miles  
**Minimum Employees:** 50  
**Goal:** Pitch bulk meal packages to HR/Benefits decision-makers for employee wellness programs

---

## How to Use This List
1. Lead with the **employee meal perk angle** — healthy, macro-tracked meals delivered weekly
2. Best contacts are **HR Directors, VP of Benefits, or Office/Facilities Managers**
3. Reference: *"10 chef-prepared meals for $9.99 each — no subscription, free delivery across NJ"*
4. Offer a **corporate sample box or team trial** as an icebreaker
